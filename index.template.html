Don’t lose your files Keep a second copy of anything important on another drive or in the cloud. Use “Safely Remove” before unplugging, never pull the USB out while files are saving, and don’t format this drive. A forgotten password or a lost authenticator locks DPLocker for good.

Choose your USB drive

DPLocker keeps your files only on a USB drive, never on this PC. Pick the drive to open.

Protect DPLocker

Set a password and link an authenticator. You’ll need both every time you open DPLocker.

Before you start: there is no password recovery. Write your password down somewhere safe and keep your authenticator. Keep a second copy of important files, because a USB drive can be lost or fail.
