DPLocker (portable)
by DATAL Projects, https://datalprojects.com

1. Copy DPLocker.exe to your USB flash drive.
2. Open it from the USB drive. It refuses to run from a PC's internal drive.
3. Set a password and link an authenticator app the first time.

Your files are encrypted in a "DPLocker Data" folder next to DPLocker.exe.
Without your password they cannot be read, even by copying the drive.

Don't lose your files:
- Keep a second copy of anything important somewhere else.
- Use "Safely Remove" before unplugging the USB drive.
- There is no password recovery. Keep your password and authenticator safe.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). DPLocker is open source under the MIT License;
